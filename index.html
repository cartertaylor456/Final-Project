The Stock Market

The stock market is a place where people buy and sell ownership shares of companies. These ownership shares are called stocks. When someone buys a stock, they own a small part of that company.

Companies sell stock to raise money for things like expanding their business, creating new products, or paying off debt. Investors buy stocks hoping the value will increase over time.

Why the Stock Market Is Important

The stock market plays a big role in the economy. It allows companies to grow and gives people opportunities to build wealth over time.

  • Helps businesses raise money
    1. helps a business to kickstart their funding
    2. leads to faster growth for young companies
  • Allows individuals to invest for retirement
  • Reflects how the economy is performing
